Privacy Policy for GutLog

Effective date: 26 August 2026
Last updated: 26 August 2026

GutLog is provided by The Pixelator (“the developer,” “we,” or “us”).

This Privacy Policy explains how GutLog handles information when you use the Android application.

1. Privacy at a glance

GutLog is designed as a private, local health and lifestyle diary.

  • Your diary and profile information is stored locally on your device.
  • GutLog does not create user accounts.
  • GutLog does not upload your diary to the developer or to a GutLog server.
  • GutLog does not use advertising, analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services.
  • GutLog does not sell, rent, or trade personal or health information.
  • The current version of GutLog does not request Internet access.
  • Any export or sharing of information happens only when you initiate it.

Because the developer does not receive your diary database, the developer cannot view it, recover it, reset its backup password, or restore it if it is lost.

2. Information you may enter

GutLog allows you to enter personal, health, wellness, and lifestyle information. Depending on the features you use, this may include:

Profile and preferences

  • A name or nickname; your real name is not required
  • Age
  • Sex
  • Height and weight
  • General activity level
  • Personal hydration and sleep targets
  • Allergies
  • Chronic conditions
  • Regular therapies, medicines, or supplements
  • App language and other settings

Food, drink, and hydration

  • Meals, foods, ingredients, and meal times
  • Notes about food or digestion
  • Water intake and drink amounts
  • Caffeine and other drinks
  • Alcohol type, amount, and alcohol percentage

Symptoms and digestive information

  • Symptoms and symptom severity
  • Pain level, location, and timing
  • Bloating, nausea, reflux, discomfort, and similar observations
  • Stool information, including Bristol Stool Scale selections
  • Notes and timestamps

Medicines, therapies, and supplements

  • Medicine or supplement names
  • Dosage or amount entered by you
  • Therapy presets
  • Times at which a medicine, supplement, or therapy was logged

Blood pressure and related measurements

  • Systolic blood pressure
  • Diastolic blood pressure
  • Pulse
  • Measurement date and time
  • Notes entered with the measurement

Exercise and movement

  • Exercise type, such as walking, running, cycling, gym, stretching, or sport
  • Duration and effort level
  • Optional distance and step count
  • Strength-training body area and set count
  • Date and time of the activity

Sleep

  • Bedtime and wake time
  • Sleep duration
  • Number of awakenings
  • Naps
  • Sleep-quality rating
  • Personal sleep target

Daily check-ins

  • Energy
  • Stress
  • Mood
  • Pain
  • Overall gut comfort
  • Notes and timestamps

You decide which information to enter. GutLog does not automatically obtain health records from doctors, hospitals, wearable devices, Health Connect, or other applications.

3. How information is used

The information you enter is processed on your device to provide the application’s features, including:

  • Displaying your diary and history
  • Producing daily and weekly summaries
  • Showing charts and chronological trends
  • Calculating hydration estimates
  • Calculating BMI estimates
  • Producing alcohol-related estimates
  • Summarizing exercise minutes and strength-training days
  • Calculating a personal training-load trend
  • Showing sleep duration, average sleep, regularity, and estimated sleep debt
  • Comparing recent results with your personal baseline
  • Identifying possible associations between sleep, stress, exercise, food, drinks, alcohol, caffeine, and symptoms
  • Creating user-requested JSON, CSV, or PDF reports

These calculations take place locally on your device.

GutLog does not use your information for advertising, profiling, credit decisions, insurance decisions, employment decisions, or automated medical decisions.

4. Estimates and pattern analysis

Some GutLog features calculate estimates or identify statistical patterns from the information you enter.

These may include hydration estimates, BMI, alcohol estimates, sleep debt, training load, activity-equivalent minutes, personal averages, and symptom correlations.

These results are informational planning tools only. They may be incomplete or inaccurate because they depend on the information entered by you and on simplified formulas.

A correlation or pattern shown by GutLog does not establish that one event caused another. GutLog does not diagnose conditions or make medical decisions.

5. Local storage

GutLog’s main database is stored in the application’s private storage area on your Android device.

Other applications normally cannot access this storage unless the device’s security has been compromised, modified, or bypassed.

Android cloud backup is disabled for GutLog. GutLog does not intentionally send its private application database to Google Drive or another cloud-backup service.

Your information normally remains on your device until:

  • You delete an individual entry;
  • You use GutLog’s “Delete all data” function;
  • You clear GutLog’s application data through Android settings; or
  • You uninstall GutLog.

Copies that you previously exported or shared are separate and will not automatically be deleted when you delete information from GutLog.

6. Encrypted local backup

GutLog can create an optional encrypted backup in its private local storage.

The current backup system uses password-based encryption. The password is used to derive an encryption key and is not saved by GutLog. The developer does not receive the password or encryption key.

If you forget the password, neither GutLog nor the developer can recover the encrypted backup.

The local encrypted backup is removed when you use “Delete all data” or uninstall the application. This does not remove copies you may have created or transferred outside GutLog.

7. Exports and sharing

GutLog can generate JSON, CSV, and PDF files on your device.

When you export a file, Android allows you to select where it will be saved. The selected destination could be local storage or an external application or service, such as a file manager or cloud-storage provider.

GutLog can also prepare a PDF report for sharing through an email or messaging application selected by you. GutLog does not send the report automatically.

A temporary report file may remain in the application’s cache until it is cleared by GutLog, Android, or the user, or until the application is uninstalled.

After information is exported or shared, its handling is controlled by the destination, recipient, and relevant third-party service. Their privacy policies and security practices apply to those copies.

You should check a report before sharing it because it may contain sensitive health and lifestyle information.

8. Information not collected by the developer

Through the current version of GutLog, the developer does not collect or receive:

  • Your diary entries
  • Your profile information
  • Health measurements
  • Symptoms
  • Food or drink records
  • Exercise or sleep records
  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Microphone or camera data
  • Browsing activity
  • Usage analytics
  • Crash-reporting data

GutLog contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or developer-operated cloud synchronization service.

9. Contacting the developer

If you voluntarily contact the developer by email or another support channel, the developer may receive information such as:

  • Your email address or contact details
  • The content of your message
  • Screenshots, reports, or other files you choose to attach
  • Basic technical information you voluntarily provide

This information will be used to respond to your request, investigate a reported problem, protect the application or its users, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Support correspondence may be stored by the developer’s email provider for as long as reasonably necessary for these purposes. Do not attach diary exports or sensitive medical information unless it is necessary and you are comfortable sharing it.

10. Third-party services

GutLog relies on Android system components for functions such as displaying the application, selecting files, saving exports, and opening the Android sharing interface.

When you choose an external email, messaging, storage, or file-management application, that third party may process the information you send to it according to its own privacy policy.

Google Play may independently process information relating to application installation, updates, purchases, security, or store usage. That processing is controlled by Google and is governed by Google’s own privacy policies. GutLog does not receive your private diary database through Google Play.

11. Security

GutLog uses measures intended to reduce unauthorized access, including:

  • Android application-private storage
  • Disabled Android cloud backup
  • Password-based authenticated encryption for the optional local backup
  • User-initiated exports and sharing
  • No developer-operated server containing user diaries
  • No Internet permission in the current version

However, no device or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Your device passcode, operating-system updates, installed applications, physical access to the device, and any exports you create all affect the security of your information.

For additional protection, use a secure device lock, keep Android updated, and store exported files carefully.

12. Retention and deletion

Diary and profile information remains on your device until you delete it or remove the application.

GutLog provides controls for:

  • Editing entries
  • Deleting individual entries
  • Deleting all application data
  • Deleting the local encrypted backup as part of “Delete all data”

Because GutLog has no user accounts and does not receive your diary database, there is no remote account or server copy for the developer to delete.

Files saved outside GutLog and reports shared with other applications or people must be deleted separately from their respective locations.

13. Your choices

You control:

  • Which information you enter
  • Whether you use a nickname instead of your real name
  • Whether you create an encrypted backup
  • Whether you create an export
  • Where an export is saved
  • Which application or person receives a shared report
  • Which individual entries are edited or deleted
  • Whether all local data is deleted

You may stop all further local processing by deleting GutLog’s data or uninstalling the application.

14. International data transfers

GutLog does not transmit your diary or profile information to the developer, so the developer does not perform an international transfer of that information.

If you export information to a cloud service, email provider, messaging application, or recipient in another country, that third party may process the information in other countries under its own terms and privacy policy.

15. Children’s privacy

GutLog is intended for adults aged 18 or older. It is not directed to children, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.

Because the application has no accounts and does not transmit diary information to the developer, the developer normally has no way to identify the age of a person using an installed copy.

16. Medical disclaimer

GutLog is a personal diary and general wellness application. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition.

Information, calculations, charts, correlations, and estimates shown by GutLog are not substitutes for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Contact a qualified healthcare professional about symptoms, measurements, medicines, alcohol use, sleep problems, exercise safety, or other health concerns. Seek urgent medical assistance when appropriate.

17. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when GutLog’s features, data handling, legal obligations, or distribution requirements change.

The updated version will display a revised “Last updated” date. When reasonably practical, material changes will also be communicated within the application or its store listing.

If a future version introduces features such as optional cloud synchronization, online accounts, analytics, advertising, or Health Connect access, this Privacy Policy and the relevant in-app disclosures will be updated before those features are made available.
